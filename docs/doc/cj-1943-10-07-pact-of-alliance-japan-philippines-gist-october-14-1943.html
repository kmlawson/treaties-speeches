Pact of Alliance between Japan and the Philippines (gist as announced), October 14, 1943

Type
Treaty
Date
14 October 1943
Parties
Japan · Philippines
Printed in
Contemporary Japan, October, 1943, pp. 1335-1336
Section
Documentary Material - The Philippines Gains Independence
View the scan on the Internet Archive →
p. 1335

In the preamble it is stated that His Majesty the Emperor of Japan and the President of the Philippines, now that Japan has decided to recognize the Philippines as an independent State, being determined that the two countries shall closely co-operate, each as good neighbour respecting the self-asserting independence of the other, to establish a greater East Asia based on justice,

p. 1336

thereby contributing to the peace of the world and being determined firmly and unshakably to eradicate all sources of evil in the way, have resolved for that purpose to conclude a Pact of Alliance.

In the Articles it is stipulated that:

1. There will be between the two countries perpetual relations of good neighbour and amity on the basis of mutual respect for sovereignty and territories.

2. The two countries will closely co-operate on matters, political, economic and military for the successful prosecution of the war of greater East Asia.

3. The two countries will closely co-operate with each other for the establishment of a greater East Asia.

4. The matters of detail necessary for the execution of the present pact will be determined through consultations between the authorities concerned of the two countries.

5. The pact will come into force as from the date on which the two countries shall have completed its ratification.

6. The pact will be ratified as soon as possible and the ratifications will be exchanged at Manila as soon as possible.

There are, moreover, Terms of Understanding attached to this pact, as follows:

The principal modality of the close military co-operation for the successful prosecution of the war of greater East Asia shall be that the Philippines will afford all kinds of facilities for the military actions to be undertaken by Japan and that both Japan and the Philippines will closely co-operate with each other in order to safeguard the territorial integrity and the independence of the Philippines.

About this collection

This site gathers the verbatim texts of treaties, laws, speeches and other official documents printed in two English-language periodicals published in wartime Japan: the Tokyo Gazette (1937–1942), a monthly digest of government reports issued under the supervision of the Cabinet Information Bureau, and Contemporary Japan: A Review of Far Eastern Affairs (issues of 1941–1945), published by the Foreign Affairs Association of Japan, whose “Documentary Material” section reprinted official texts.

It also includes the “Texts of Treaties, Agreements, Notes and Statutes of the Powers Concerning Manchuria” (1895–1932) printed as Supplement III of the Japan-Manchoukuo Year Book 1934 (the first of a section of texts from year books), and, from the two periodicals, selected government programmes, reports, statistical articles, chronologies and profiles of public figures.

Only texts printed in full in the journals are included; commentary, summaries and articles about them are not. The issues covered are those indexed on the Tokyo Gazette table of contents and Contemporary Japan pages at Frog in a Well, and scanned on the Internet Archive.

These are official translations and statements of the wartime Japanese state and its allies, and should be read as such.
