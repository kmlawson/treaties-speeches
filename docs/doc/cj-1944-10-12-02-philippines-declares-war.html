The Philippines Declares War on Britain and the United States, September 23, 1944

Type
Document
Date
23 September 1944
Speaker / issued by
Jose Laurel, President of the Republic of the Philippines
Printed in
Contemporary Japan, October - December, 1944, pp. 1070-1071
Section
Documentary Material
View the scan on the Internet Archive →
p. 1070

Whereas the Filipino people during the whole period of subjection to alien rule unremittingly laboured for freedom and independence and to this end fought two wars and countless revolutions; whereas upon attainment by the Philippines of her cherished goal of freedom and independence and on the occasion of the inauguration of the Republic of Philippines on October 14, 1943, the President appealed to all nations and peoples of the world for amity and goodwill and, to the United States of America especially pleaded that the Philippines be spared suffering and destruction incident to the resumption of military operations on our soil; whereas notwithstanding the appeal, the United States of America and Great Britain attacked from the air certain parts of the Philippines, thereby violating the territorial integrity of the Republic and causing death or injury to citizens and destruction or damage to property; whereas the Philippines must safeguard its independence and territorial integrity as every self-respecting sovereign State is in honour bound to do; and whereas the Republic of the Philippines entered into the pact of alliance with Japan based on mutual respect of sovereignty and territories to safeguard territorial integrity and independence of the Philippines:

p. 1071

Now, therefore, I, Jose Laurel, President of the Republic of the Philippines, do hereby proclaim that a state of war exists between the Republic of the Philippines and the United States of America and Great Britain effective 10 a.m. September 23, 1944.

About this collection

This site gathers the verbatim texts of treaties, laws, speeches and other official documents printed in two English-language periodicals published in wartime Japan: the Tokyo Gazette (1937–1942), a monthly digest of government reports issued under the supervision of the Cabinet Information Bureau, and Contemporary Japan: A Review of Far Eastern Affairs (issues of 1941–1945), published by the Foreign Affairs Association of Japan, whose “Documentary Material” section reprinted official texts.

It also includes the “Texts of Treaties, Agreements, Notes and Statutes of the Powers Concerning Manchuria” (1895–1932) printed as Supplement III of the Japan-Manchoukuo Year Book 1934 (the first of a section of texts from year books), and, from the two periodicals, selected government programmes, reports, statistical articles, chronologies and profiles of public figures.

Only texts printed in full in the journals are included; commentary, summaries and articles about them are not. The issues covered are those indexed on the Tokyo Gazette table of contents and Contemporary Japan pages at Frog in a Well, and scanned on the Internet Archive.

These are official translations and statements of the wartime Japanese state and its allies, and should be read as such.
