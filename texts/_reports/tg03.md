## Incomplete texts
- 2-18 / 00 Statement of the Japanese Government: p. 16 not transcribed (cut off a worker; SKIPPAGE). p. 17 (end) transcribed.
- 2-19 / 04 American note: pp. 32, 33, 34 skipped (three SKIPPAGEs) and p. 35 cut off a further worker; marked BLOCKED. Pages 35-36 and the Japanese reply (if it is printed on later pages) are not transcribed; the file holds placeholders only. Date 1938-10-06 is inferred, not confirmed.

"The Situation in China", military reviews, economic and administrative articles (Price Control, Health Insurance, Programme for Economic Development of China, Emigration to Manchoukuo, Foreign Trade, Student Labour, Store Law, Broadcasting, etc.): commentary or articles, not verbatim texts.
