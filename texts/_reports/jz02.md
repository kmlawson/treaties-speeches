## Anomalies and bookkeeping

1. **Printed p. 1142 is not in the scan.** PDF page 1199 is a second copy of printed p. 1146 (the Siam treaty), and the page order goes 1141 (PDF 1198), 1146 (PDF 1199), 1143 (PDF 1200). I did not find 1142 at any position nearby, and I did not search the rest of the 1311-page PDF. The text after Article XVIII of Supplementary Agreement (A) (Articles XIX and XX and any signature block) is on 1142 and is missing. 01a is marked INCOMPLETE and the placeholder is in the file. The SKIPPAGE line for 01a 1142 is in the progress file.
2. **Progress file lines that do not match the format:** `PAGE .../01a... 1139R-end` (a page-end note, not a column; the 1139R cycle itself was logged separately and completed). `PAGE .../04-cultural... 1154R` was logged for the right column, whose text was transcribed into 04a, not 04. `PAGE .../01a... 1142L` was logged before I found that 1142 was missing; the page was then recorded with SKIPPAGE.
3. Printed 1146 (PDF 1199) was rendered and viewed by accident while looking for printed 1142. It was not transcribed then; the Siam treaty (03) was transcribed from PDF 1203, which is the same printed page.
4. Where a sentence runs across a printed-page break (common in 01a and 03), the `[[p. N]]` marker is on its own line between the two halves, with blank lines around it, so the sentence reads on across the marker.
