# Report tg05 (Tokyo Gazette, Aug 1939 - Jan 1940)

PDF page = leaf + 1 in all issues (checked on 3.6 and 3.7). Final pass by a resuming agent; the earlier agent's files for 3.2-3.5 and the first files of 3.6/3.7 were all marked DONE and were kept without re-reading.

## 3.2 (Aug 1939): 3 files
01 Australia trade statement, 02 Venezuela trade statement, 03 list "Treaties and Ordinances Recently Promulgated".
Rejected: Tientsin concession article, Trend of Reading, Chronological Table, Labour Power, Situation in China (all narrative/commentary/military reports).

## 3.3 (Sep 1939): 6 files
01-05 the five Documents (Foreign Office announcements/statements), 06 list. Rejected: Soviet Oppression article (commentary, checked on contact sheet), Institute of Public Health, Postal Savings, Civilians to National Services, Post Office Annuity, Situation in China.

## 3.4 (Oct 1939): 4 files
01-03 the three Documents, 04 list. Rejected: Motion Pictures, Shipping, Vocational Abilities, Civil Aviation (articles).

## 3.5 (Nov 1939): 2 files
01 list, 02 Judicial Protective Work ordinances (joint summary, about half page). Rejected: Treasury Deposit, Reabsorbing Soldiers, Korea industry, Nomonhan, Bereaved Families, Situation in China.

## 3.6 (Dec 1939): 2 files
01 Ordinance Pertaining to Control of Prices, Etc. (full text); 02 list (pdf 34-35, read this pass). Rejected: Secondary school admission, Electric Power, Exchange Basis of the Yen (Dept of Finance explanatory articles, contact sheet checked), Technical Experts, Situation in China. The other entries in the list column are summaries only; the Land and House Rents full text is in 3.7 file 03.

## 3.7 (Jan 1940): 4 files
01 Extracts from Ordinance No. 53 of the Dept of Commerce and Industry (Articles 1-3 + appended table, printed pp. 258-260 as a footnote in the Yen-Bloc exports article; found and transcribed this pass, pdf 14-16). 02 Foreign Office spokesman statement on British blockade. 03 Ordinance Pertaining to Control of Land and House Rent. 04 list (pdf 34-36 read this pass).
Rejected: Labour conditions, Temporary Measures concerning Exports (article itself is commentary; only the ordinance extracts taken), Home Education, Wholesale and Retail Trade, Situation in China.

## Illegible spots
None. Notes: 3.6 list prints "No. 703" for the Land and House Rents ordinance (index says 704); kept as printed with [sic] in the table and a note.
