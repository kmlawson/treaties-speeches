[[p. 962]]

Croatia, recently recognized by the Japanese Government, having decided to participate in the Three-Power Pact, concluded among Japan, Germany and Italy, a protocol concerning her participation was signed at Venice, on June 15, by the plenipotentiaries representing the three Powers and by the Croatian plenipotentiary.

The contents of the protocol are similar to those of the protocols signed at the time of the participation of Hungary and others.
