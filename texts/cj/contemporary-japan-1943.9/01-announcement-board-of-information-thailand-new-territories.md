[[p. 1210]]

Following the conversations between General Hidéki Tohjo, Prime Minister of Japan, and Field Marshal P. Phibul Songgram, Prime Minister of Thailand, on July 4 of this year, the Governments of the two countries have been engaged in negotiations for the conclusion of a treaty between Japan and Thailand concerning the territories of Thailand in the Malay and Shan regions. Having reached an agreement on the draft treaty, the treaty was signed today, August 20, 1943, at Bangkok between Mr. Teiji Tsubokami, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of Japan to Thailand, and Field Marshal P. Phibul Songgram, Prime Minister and acting Minister of Foreign Affairs:---
