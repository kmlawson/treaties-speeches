[[p. 1211]]

The Government of the Empire of Japan and the Government of Greater Germany jointly and solemnly declare as follows:

The treachery of the Government of Marshal Badoglio affects in no way the Three-Power Pact, which remains in force without the slightest change.

The Government of the Empire of Japan and the Government of Greater Germany are determined, jointly with all the measures at their disposal, to carry on the war to the victorious conclusion.
