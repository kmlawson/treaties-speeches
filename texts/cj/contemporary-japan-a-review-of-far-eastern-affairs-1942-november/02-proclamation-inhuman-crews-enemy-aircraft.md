[[p. 1669]]

If members of the crew of enemy aircraft who raid the territory of Japan and come within the reaches of our authority are found to have committed a cruel and inhuman act, the same shall be tried before the military court and punished with death or severe penalty.

The same shall apply to those who make an air raid on Manchoukuo or areas of our military operations and come within the reaches of our authority.
