[[p. 843]]

The General Commission under the Tripartite Pact held its meeting at 4 : 30 p.m. May 9, at the official residence of the Foreign Minister, under the chairmanship of H. E. Mr. Matsuoka. Their Excellencies General Ott and

[[p. 844]]

Monsieur Indelli took part in the meeting. Questions relating to the co-operation among the three Powers under the Tripartite Pact, including economic matters, were brought up and fully discussed. It was agreed that the commission shall sit as often as possible with the assistance of the Military and Economic Commissions and continue its work in close co-operation with the commissions established in Berlin and Rome.
