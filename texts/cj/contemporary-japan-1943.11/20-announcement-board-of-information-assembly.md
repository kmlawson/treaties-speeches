[[p. 1340]]

"The countries of greater East Asia which have been striving vigorously for the attainment of their common objective on the firm basis of neighbourly friendship, mutual assistance and co-operation have decided, in order to carry on frank discussions among their representatives concerning the successful prosecution of the war of greater East Asia and the plan for the construction of a greater East Asia, to hold an Assembly of greater East Asiatic nations in Tokyo.

"The names of the representatives, associates and observer are as follows:
