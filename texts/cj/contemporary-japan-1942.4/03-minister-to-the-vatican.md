MINISTER TO THE VATICAN APPOINTED: STATEMENT OF THE FOREIGN OFFICE, MARCH 26, 1942

With a view to rendering still closer the existing amicable relations between Japan and the Holy See, the Jananese [sic] Government have decided to delegate Minister Ken Harada specially to the State of the City of Vatican. In view of the present world situation as well as the presence in greater East Asia of numerous adherents of the Roman Catholic faith, the significance of promoting friendly relations and mutual contacts between Japan and the Holy See hardly requires any explanation.
