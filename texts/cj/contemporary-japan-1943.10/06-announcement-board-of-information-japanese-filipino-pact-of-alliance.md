[[p. 1335]]

The Philippines has proclaimed her independence today, October 14, 1943. Japan has given immediate recognition to that country, while a Pact of Alliance between Japan and the Philippines has been signed by Mr. Shozo Murata, Japanese Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary, and Mr. Claro M. Recto, Minister of the State and Plenipotentiary of the Republic of the Philippines.
