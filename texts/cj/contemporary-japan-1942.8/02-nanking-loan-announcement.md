### 100 Million Yen Loan to Nanking

### (1) Announcement by the Board of Information, July 28, 1942

With a view to contributing toward the sound development of the note-issuing system of the Central Reserve Bank of China, the Japanese Government has in response to the desire of the National Government of China, decided to grant a loan of ¥100,000,000 and the contract with respect to the said loan has been signed on July 28 between the Bank of Japan and the Central Reserve Bank of China.
