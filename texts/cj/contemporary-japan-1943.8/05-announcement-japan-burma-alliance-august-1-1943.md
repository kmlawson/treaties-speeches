[[p. 1086]]

Burma has proclaimed her independence today, August 1, 1943, and has immediately declared war on the United States and Britain.

Japan has given immediate recognition to that country and has had a Treaty of Alliance Between Japan and Burma signed between Mr. Renzo Sawada, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of Japan to Burma, and Dr. Ba Maw, Prime Minister of Burma.
