[[p. 648]]

Mengchiang has greatly contributed to our war efforts by supplying both North China and Japan with the materials secured from the rich mines, farms and pastures of the north, while it occupies a most important position from all angles.

Since its inception in 1937, the Bank of Mengchiang has obtained marked results in developing the monetary system of the region. It will now be connected with the monetary system of Japan to the extent of ¥100,000,000. This will consolidate the foundation of the bank and help strengthen the monetary system of Mengchiang. The Japanese Government intends to accord it every help, so that the monetary system of the region may progress and contribute to the stabilization of the living of the people.
