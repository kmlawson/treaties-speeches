[[p. 844]]

With a view to contributing to the healthy development of the Centra [sic] Reserve Bank of China notes, the Japanesse [sic] Government have decided to extend a credit of ¥400,000,000 to the Central Reserve Bank of China in addition to the loan of ¥100,000,000 which was extended to it by the Bank of Japan in July of 1942.
