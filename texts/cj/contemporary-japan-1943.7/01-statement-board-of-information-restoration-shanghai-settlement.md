[[p. 931]]

The Japanese Government, in accordance with Article 4 of the Agreement Between Japan and the Republic of China Concerning the Rendition of Concessions and the Relinquishment of Extra-territorial Rights concluded on January 9 this year, had their Ambassador to China carry on consultations with the Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of China relative to the enforcement of the restoration of administrative rights in the International Settlement of Shanghai to the National Government of the Republic of China. Complete agreement of views having been reached, the Agreement and Terms of Understanding for the enforcement of the restoration of the administrative rights in the International Settlement of Shanghai were signed at Nanking today, June 30.
