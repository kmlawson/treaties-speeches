[[p. 28]]

1. Both the Japanese and the Soviet forces shall cease strictly all hostile actions in their present positions.

2. The bodies of the Japanese and the Soviet soldiers killed and left in the zone between their positions shall be recovered.

3. The positions held at present by the Japanese and the Soviet forces shall be mutually confirmed at noon, August 12th.

4. Documents concerning the foregoing three items shall be exchanged between the two sides.
