[[p. 29]]

1. The representatives of the Japanese and the Soviet troops shall notify their respective governments concerning the positions held by them on the north side of Changkufeng ridge (the Japanese and Soviet troops were then 5 metres apart).

2. The commanders of the Japanese and the Soviet troops shall, in accordance with the decisions of the Moscow and Tokyo governments regarding the cessation of hostilities, guarantee that they will take all possible measures to prevent further trouble of any sort in the neighbourhood of Changkufeng.

3. Commencing at 10 p. m., August 12, 1938, the main bodies of the Japanese and the Soviet forces on the north side of the Changkufeng ridge shall retire to lines 80 metres from the ridge.
