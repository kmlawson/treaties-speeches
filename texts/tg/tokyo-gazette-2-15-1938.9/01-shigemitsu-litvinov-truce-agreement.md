[[p. 28]]

1. Both the Japanese and the Soviet forces shall cease hostilities at noon of August 11th, Soviet Maritime Province time.

2. Both the Japanese and the Soviet forces shall maintain their respective lines held by them at midnight, August 10th, Soviet Maritime Province time. As regards the method of carrying out the terms of the agreement, it was decided that it be determined by negotiations between representatives of the two forces on the front.
