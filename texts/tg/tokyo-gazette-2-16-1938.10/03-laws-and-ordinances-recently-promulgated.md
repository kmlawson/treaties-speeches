notes: All entries are short summaries, several headings sharing one paragraph; none prints the text. Page number not printed on p. 48 (first page of the column). Most entries are dated in the form "No. 525 of July 29, 1938" without the word "Promulgated". Imperial Ordinances Nos. 609 and 610 are given with the year only. Imperial Ordinance No. 528 is printed as "of July 27, 1938" although the three ordinances before it are of July 29. The summary of Treaty No. 6 says it "was promulgated together with supplementary documents on August 31, 1937" (sic, heading says 1938) "and put in force as from September 1st". Other dates of operation given in the summaries: Law for Agrarian Adjustment put into operation August 1, 1938; badge ordinance in force September 1st; electricity laws in force August 10th; Aeroplane Industry Law in force August 30th.
