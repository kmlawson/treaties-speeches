[[p. 9]]

In compliance with the request of the Government of Great Britain, made with the approbation of the Government of the United States of America, the Royal Government proposes to the Signatory States of the Treaty of February 6, 1922, to convene at Brussels on the 30th of this month with the view of examining in accordance with Article VII of that Treaty, the situation in the Far East and of studying the peaceful means of hastening the end of the unfortunate conflict existing there.

Tokyo, October 20, 1937
