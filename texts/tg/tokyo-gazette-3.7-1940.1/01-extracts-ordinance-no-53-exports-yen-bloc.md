**Extracts from Ordinance No. 53 of the Department of Commerce and Industry of September 20, 1939: the Regulations Pertaining to Control of Exports to the Leased Territory of Kwantung, Manchoukuo and China**

**Article 1.** The articles designated by the Minister of Commerce and Industry shall not be exported to the Leased Territory of Kwantung, Manchoukuo or China unless approval is obtained of the organizations designated by the Minister of Commerce and Industry (hereinafter referred to as the associations for control of exports by commodities); but this provision shall not apply to cases where approval is obtained of the Minister of Commerce and Industry by reason of special circumstances.

**Article 2.** The articles mentioned in the import tariff table, appended to the Import Tariff of Japan and mentioned in the table appended to the present ordinance, shall not be exported to the Leased Territory of Kwantung, Manchoukuo or China unless approval is obtained, in the case of articles exported by those who maintain offices in the districts under the jurisdictions of the organizations designated by the Minister of Commerce and Industry (hereinafter referred to as the associations for control of exports by districts), of the associations for control of exports by districts concerned, and, in the case of articles exported by others, of the Prefectural Governors who have jurisdictions over the places where they maintain offices; but this provision shall not apply to cases where approval is obtained of the Minister of Commerce and Industry by reason of special circumstances.

**Article 3.** The provisions of the preceding two articles shall not apply to any one of the following numbers:

1. Articles for the use of the Imperial Household.
2. Articles belonging to a foreign ruler and his family and suite, visiting Japan.
3. Articles for the personal use of foreign Ambassadors and Ministers and other envoys of similar standing accredited to Japan, and the staff of foreign Embassies or Legations or Consuls in Japan, and articles for the official use of foreign Embassies, Legations or Consulates in Japan.
4. Articles exported by governmental offices.
5. Personal effects and household articles in transit due to change of residence; and articles for ship's use.
6. Articles exported for exhibiting at an exposition.

[[p. 259]]

7. Articles exported for purposes other than sale, provided that the original value of any article does not exceed one hundred yen.
