[[p. 29]]

I have the honour to inform Your Excellency that, in view of the very friendly attitude shown by the Italian Government and people toward Japan since the outset of the present Sino-Japanese Affair, it is a particular matter of regret for the Japanese Government that Mr. Sandro Sandri, an Italian press correspondent who

[[p. 30]]

happened to be on board the American gunboat Panay on December 12th at the time the Japanese naval aircraft bombed her by mistake, passed away as the result of that bombing.

I hereby tender, on behalf of the Japanese Government, the expression of its most sincere condolence, which I request Your Excellency to transmit to your Government. I wish to add, Your Excellency, that the Japanese Government is prepared to bear full responsibilities in making the necessary indemnification for the losses involved in this unfortunate incident.
