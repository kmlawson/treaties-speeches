[[p. 163]]

The Japanese Government have been exerting their efforts toward a settlement of various pending questions between Japan and the Soviet Union. Both the Governments of Japan and the Soviet Union have recognized that a solution of the dispute in the district of Nomonhan, along the boundary between Manchoukuo and Outer Mongolia, would dispel the unpleasant atmosphere prevailing between the two countries, and would contribute toward the restoration of Japanese-Soviet relations to their normal status. Therefore

[[p. 164]]

Mr. Shigenori Togo, Japanese Ambassador in Moscow, and Mr. Viacheslav Molotov, People's Commissar for Foreign Affairs, recently have held several conversations for this purpose. As a result of these interviews, an agreement for the cessation of hostilities has been concluded between the two countries on the 15th of September.
