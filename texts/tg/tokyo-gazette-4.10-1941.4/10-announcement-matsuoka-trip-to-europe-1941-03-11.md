[[p. 426]]

Foreign Minister Mr. Matsuoka will shortly visit Germany and Italy to exchange personal felicitations with and meet the leaders of Germany and Italy in connection with the conclusion of the Tripartite Pact. The views regarding the intended trip were exchanged between the three Governments at the time of the conclusion of the Treaty but the duties of the Foreign Minister have so far prevented him from acting thereon.

The Minister will incidentally avail himself of the opportunity to make a personal observation, on the spot, of the situation in Europe.
