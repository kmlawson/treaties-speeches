[[p. 427]]

The understanding between Japan and Venezuela by which Japan undertook since July 1, 1939, to purchase as much of Venezuelan products as possible and Venezuela agreed not to adopt unreasonably restrictive measures against Japanese goods for reason of unequilibrium of trade between the two countries expired at the end of February of this year. The Japanese Government have conducted, in view of the importance of Venezuela as a market for our exports, negotiations with the Venezuelan Government through our representatives there for the conclusion of a new understanding for similar purpose. As a result, an understanding was reached on March 11, by which the two countries will adjust their trade relations for a year beginning March 11, on the basis of the understanding concluded on July 1, 1939.
