[[p. 25]]

As a result of recent negotiations between the Japanese Ambassador at Moscow, Mr. Shigenori Togo, and the Soviet People's Commissar for Foreign Affairs, Mr. Viacheslav Molotov, an agreement was reached on June 9 with mutual recognition of interests of both parties—Japan and Manchoukuo, and the Soviet Union and Outer Mongolia—on the precise demarcation of the frontier in the area of last year's conflict, a question which in due time was not solved by the Mixed Commission composed of the delegates of Japan and Manchoukuo, the Soviet Union and Outer Mongolia for the precise demarcation of frontier in the said area and which has formed an obstacle to the regulation of relations of Japan with the Soviet Union and of Manchoukuo with Outer Mongolia.
