[[p. 80]]

By the understanding reached last year between Japanese and American interests concerned, the Association of Japanese Cotton Piece-goods Exporters to East Asia has been voluntarily restricting the amount of cotton piece-goods exported to the Philippines to 45,000,000 square metres for the period from August 1 of last year to the end of July of this year. In view of the approaching expiration of such period of voluntary limitation

[[p. 81]]

of export, negotiations were conducted between the Japanese and American cotton interests through the good offices of the Japanese Embassy at Washington. These negotiations have satisfactorily been concluded, as a result of which it has been decided to restrict voluntarily the amount of cotton piece-goods to be exported to the Philippines during the period of one year from August 1 this year to 45,000,000 square metres.
