[[p. 206]]

Mr. Ichizo Kobayashi, who was sent to the Netherlands East Indies for the special purpose of conducting negotiations with that Dutch colony, has decided to return temporarily to Japan as it is necessary for him to be present at the ceremonies in commemoration of the 2,600th anniversary of the founding of the Japanese Empire and also to make a report to, and consult with, the Government concerning his negotiations.

During the absence of Mr. Kobayashi, the negotiations at Batavia, which are progressing smoothly, will, of course, be continued between Consul General, Mr. Otoji Saito and Mr. Tomotsune Ota and the Netherlands East Indies delegation.
