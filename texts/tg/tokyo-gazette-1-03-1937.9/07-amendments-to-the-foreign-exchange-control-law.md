[[p. 45]]

The importance of maintaining the exchange value of the yen at the present level need hardly be stressed. For the maintenance and stabilization of the exchange rates, the Foreign Exchange Control Law has now been amended, whereby provisions are made for the control of the acquisition or

[[p. 46]]

disposal of claims and obligations expressed in terms of Japanese currency by parties resident in foreign countries, or for the control of the acquisition or disposal of property in foreign countries. Further amendments have been made to render the control of secret exports of gold even more strict.
