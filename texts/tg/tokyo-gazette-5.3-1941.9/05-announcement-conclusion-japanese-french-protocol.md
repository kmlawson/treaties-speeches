[[p. 165]]

With regard to the conclusion of the Protocol between Japan and France concerning Joint Defence of French Indo-China, necessary procedures were taken, and yesterday, July 28, an Imperial Sanction was given thereto. The Government, therefore, immediately sent instructions to the Ambassador to France, Mr. Sotomatsu Kato, to sign the Protocol, which was duly signed and sealed at Vichy today, July 29, by the Ambassador and the French Vice-Premier and concurrently Foreign Minister, Admiral Jean-François Darlan, and it went into effect on the same date.
